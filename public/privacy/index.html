Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Noted Path LLC ("Noted Path", "we", "us") runs notedpath.com. This policy explains what information we handle when you visit this website or contact us.

What this policy covers

This policy covers notedpath.com only. Our products run at their own addresses and can collect different information. Read the terms and privacy information for each product before you use it.

Information we collect

This website has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising. We handle information in two cases:

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider automatically records technical data for each request, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time of the request. The provider uses this data to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Email you send us. If you write to us, we receive your email address and everything you put in your message.

Cookies and similar technology

This website does not set cookies and does not use browser storage or tracking pixels. It does not load fonts, scripts or other content from third parties. For this reason, the site does not show a cookie banner. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

How we use information

We use server logs to operate and protect the website, and we use your email to reply to you. Our legal basis, where one is required, is our legitimate interest in running a secure website and in answering messages you send us.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We share information only with service providers that help us run this website and our email, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep information

We keep email for as long as we need it to answer you and to keep reasonable business records. Our hosting provider keeps server logs for a limited period under its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information. To make a request, email hello@notedpath.com. We will not treat you differently for making a request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

International visitors

We are based in the United States. Your information can be processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date.
