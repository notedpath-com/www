# Brand assets

The masters. Not deployed: only `public/` is published.

- `logo.png` — transparent background. The source for anything drawn on the
  site's own dark surfaces, such as the nav mark.
- `logo-black.png` — on black. The source for the favicon and app icons, where
  the tile needs its own background.
- `logo-white.png` — on white, for anywhere that needs a light surface.

Those three are 1254x1254 with the mark occupying roughly the middle 820x676,
so everything generated from them is cropped in before being resized.

The lockup, mark plus wordmark, arrived as one sheet with both variants stacked
and is split by the script:

- `lockup-variations.png` — the sheet as supplied. Note its top half is not
  actually transparent: the checkerboard is painted into the pixels.
- `lockup.png` — the top half, with that checkerboard keyed out to real alpha.
  Black wordmark, so it belongs on light surfaces.
- `lockup-black.png` — the bottom half, white wordmark on black. This is what
  the site's nav uses, at 36px tall.

## Palette

Noted Path logo, Option 4 (Blue & Mint Green).

| Colour | Hex | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bright blue | `#31C1FD` | Left figure and the upward arrow |
| Mint green | `#34F0B1` | Right figure |
| Black | `#000000` | Background option |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Background option |

Take the hex from this table, not from the PNGs: sampling the raster picks up
edge blending and lands a shade off (`#31C1FC`, `#34F0B2`).

Note that the site's own accent is still `#7DA2FF`, a periwinkle blue used for
every link, card icon, path and dot. It does not come from this palette.

`public/og-image.png` is not generated by the script: it is a hand-made card
with no master. Its 76px logo tile was painted over and replaced in place at
(80, 72). Redo that by hand if the logo changes again.

Regenerate `public/` icons with `node brand/icons.mjs` (needs `npx sharp-cli`
or a local sharp install; it is a one-off tool, not a project dependency).
